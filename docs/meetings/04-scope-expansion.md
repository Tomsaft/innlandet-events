# Meeting 4: Scope expansion

**Date:** 19.10.2026 | **Attendees:** Tord & Steffen | **Format:** online

**Decision:** Widen the scope from students in Hamar to all citizens in Innlandet. Add two additional data
sources:
- Entur (public transport): how to get to an event
- Yr (weather): what the weather will be

**Goal:** People see what is happening where, how to get there, and what
the weather will be.
