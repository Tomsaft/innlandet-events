# Meeting 4: Scope expansion

**Date:** [date, before 2026-10-20] | **Attendees:** [names]

**Decision:** Widen the scope from Hamar to all of Innlandet, and add two data
sources:
- Entur (public transport): how to get to an event
- Yr (weather): what the weather will be

**Goal:** Students see what is happening where, how to get there, and what
the weather will be.
